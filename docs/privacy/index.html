Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026 · Applies to the NutriPulse mobile app (Android & iOS)

NutriPulse is a fully offline app. All of your data — diet logs, body metrics, targets, and step counts — is stored locally on your device only.

We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

NutriPulse does not collect or transmit any data to us or to any third party. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Health and fitness data (diet entries, body metrics, step counts)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash diagnostics

2. Local Data Storage

All information you enter into the app (food records, body metrics such as height, weight, age and gender, macro targets, and step logs) is saved in a local database on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device unless you personally choose to share it (for example, via a system share sheet).
  • Is deleted permanently if you uninstall the app.
  • Is not readable by us, because we operate no servers and no cloud backend.

3. Health & Fitness Data (Health Connect / HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, the app can read today's step count from your device health service (Google Health Connect on Android, Apple HealthKit on iOS) to estimate daily active energy.

  • The app requests this permission only when you tap the sync control.
  • Step data is read-only, used for local calculations, and is never uploaded, shared, or synced anywhere.
  • You can revoke access at any time in your device's health permission settings; the app will continue to work with manual step entry.

4. Permissions

The app requests only the minimum permissions needed for its features:

  • Health steps read access (optional) — to sync today's step count, as described in Section 3.
  • Vibration — for subtle haptic feedback during interactions.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data collection occurs at all, the app is compliant with children's privacy regulations such as COPPA.

6. Medical Disclaimer

NutriPulse provides general nutrition and activity estimates for informational and educational purposes only. It is not medical advice, does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and is not a substitute for professional healthcare. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any diet or exercise program.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because the app works fully offline, any future version that changes data handling will be clearly disclosed here and in the app's store listing before release.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@nutripulse.app.
